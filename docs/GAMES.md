# Bip Island — Mini-games & Freshness Plan

Agreed 1 October 2026. Live copy: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/b9e0e92e-289f-4afe-a5f7-2b4d4bf8460c

Bip Island gets 28 mini-games across the four islands, built as reusable game types fed by content lists, so every round is different and new games and surprises can be added with each update without touching the rest.

## How freshness works

Novelty comes from five layers stacked on top of each other, so even a child who plays daily keeps meeting something new, while the learning underneath stays steady.

| Layer | What the child notices | How it works | Effort to build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. New rounds every time | Different words, pictures and numbers each round | Each game builds its rounds at random from content lists, at the child's current level | Built once per game |
| 2. New worlds for old games | Bubble Pop becomes balloons at the fair, then fireflies at night | Every game has 3 or more “skins”: same rules, new setting, art and sounds | Art only |
| 3. Bip's recommendations | Bip suggests something different each visit | The child picks freely; Bip recommends a game, steering towards islands played least lately | Built once |
| 4. The island grows | New spots, paths and creatures appear on the map | Mastering a skill reveals a new area on the map, which unlocks its games | Per area |
| 5. Surprises | A mystery box, a golden round, a visitor | Daily and random events layered on top (see Rewards and surprises) | Small, ongoing |

On top of these, every app update can add a whole new game, skin or word list, marked with a “New!” flag on the map so it's the first thing the child sees.

## Coding

| Game | How it plays | Skill | Ages | Skins |

## Rewards and surprises

Rewards are things to collect: every correct answer moves the child up a ladder of stars, stickers, creatures and decorations. There is nothing to buy, no countdowns and no “come back or lose it”.

| Tier | How it's earned | What the child gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Stars | Every correct answer | A star drops into Bip's star jar |
| 2. Stickers | Every 10 stars, and every finished game | A sticker for the sticker book that matches what was learnt (a sun for “s”, seven ladybirds for 7) |
| 3. Creatures | Filling a sticker page, such as all six sounds in a group | A creature moves onto the island and stays on the map: a penguin, a tortoise, a hadeda, a crab |
| 4. Decorations | Mastering a skill, or a creature asking for a home | Something to place anywhere on the map: palm trees, a lighthouse, flags, a treehouse |
| 5. New areas | Mastering a whole group of skills | A new part of the island opens, with its own games |

Surprises sit on top of the tiers:

| Surprise | When it appears | What the child gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bip's mystery box | First visit of each day | A bonus sticker or decoration |
| Golden round | About 1 round in 15, at random | Letters or numbers turn gold and earn double stars |
| Creature visits | Every few sessions | One of the child's own creatures pops into a game to cheer them on |
| Special days | Birthday, school holidays, Heritage Day | Bip wears a party hat and a themed skin runs for the day |
| “New!” flag | After an app update | Whatever the update added glows on the map until it's tried |

The sticker book and creature collection show everything not yet earned as grey outlines, so kids can always see there's more to find.

## Choosing games and play time

Children choose freely on the map; Bip recommends but never forces.

- **Bip's pick:** one island glows with Bip's suggestion, chosen from what's due for review, a new skill that's ready, and what hasn't been played lately.
- **Balancing nudge:** if most recent play has been on one island (for example three sessions in a row on Letters), Bip suggests another: “How about some numbers? The ducks are waiting!” The child can still say no.
- **Play-time break:** after 20 minutes of play, Bip's battery runs low. He lets the child finish the current game, says goodbye, and plugs himself in to charge.
- **Cool-down:** for the next 20 minutes the game shows Bip charging and games stay closed. The break keeps counting even if the app is quit and reopened, so it can't be skipped.
- **Parents decide:** play length, break length and an optional daily maximum are set in settings behind the parent gate, where a grown-up can also end a break early.

## Making it robust

The game is split into three separate parts, so adding a new game or new words never risks breaking the ones already on your child's Mac.

1. **Content lists** (plain data files): every word with its sounds, picture and voice clip; every number set; every coding map. Adding 50 new words is a data change, not a code change.
2. **Game types**: each mini-game follows the same template. It says which skills and ages it teaches, builds a round from the content lists at the child's level, checks the answer, and lists its skins. A new game is one new file that plugs in.
3. **Bip's planner**: suggests what to play next from the child's progress, what's due for review, and what they haven't seen lately. It works the same for 10 games or 100.

**Levels inside each game.** Each game in `Content/curriculum/games.json` can list `levels`, easiest first (for example Count & Tap counts to 5, then 10, 15 and 20; Bubble Pop adds bubbles and speed; Sound Buttons moves to four- and five-sound words; Bip's Path moves to bigger grids). A child starts at the first level marked for their band, then 3 right answers in a row move the game up a level and 2 misses in a row drop it back. The level shows as stars at the top of the screen, and every visit (8 questions) ends with a celebration of the stars earned and any new level. If a level has nothing to offer yet at the child's phonics group, the game quietly uses easier material rather than ending the visit.

In code, the template is the `IMiniGame` interface in `godot/BipCore/Games/` (one file per game type, with its rounds tested in `godot/BipCore.Tests`); each game's screen is in `godot/Scripts/Games/`. A game's skills, ages and island come from its entry in `Content/curriculum/games.json`.

Automatic checks run on every update before it can reach your Mac:

- **Every round is fair:** the build plays 1,000 random rounds of every game and fails if any round has no right answer, two right answers, or a distractor that sounds the same.
- **Nothing is missing:** every word must have its voice clip and picture, or the build stops.
- **No repeats:** the same word never appears twice in one session.
- **Progress is safe:** a new version always reads your child's saved progress from the old one, and the check fails if it can't.
- **One bad game can't sink the app:** if a game fails to load, Bip quietly skips it and the rest keep working.

## Build order

The plug-in template and Bip's planner come first, so every later game is quick to add.

| Phase | Games added | Freshness added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Prototype (being built now) | Meet the Sound, Sound Hunt, Bubble Pop, on the plug-in template and reading all content from `Content/` | Random rounds |
| 2a. Core of each island | Letter Trace, Feed the Monster, Count & Tap, Quick Look, Sound Buttons, Word Builder, Morning Order, Bip's Path | Bip's recommendations, play-time breaks, sticker book, mystery box |
| 2b. Second wave | Number Train, More or Less, Missing Letter, Rhyme Time, Pattern Party, Fix-It | Skins (3 per game), golden rounds |
| 3. Polish | Letter Fishing, Sound Detective, Ten-Frame Garden, Tricky Word Memory, Silly Sentences, Repeat Robot, Bip's Dance Party | Island grows, creature collection, decorations |
| 4. Older kids | Corner Shop, Word Rocket (built 4 Oct 2026, for ages 4–8, all three skins), Puddle Rules | Special days |
| Ongoing | A new game, skin or word list with each update | “New!” flag on the map |

## Decisions

- [x] Language: proper South African English, no slang. Spaza Shop is now Corner Shop, and the braai scene is now a picnic.
- [x] Rewards: a tiered collection of stars, stickers, creatures, decorations and new areas, instead of dressing up Bip.
- [x] Choice: children pick freely; Bip recommends, and nudges towards another island when one gets most of the play.
- [x] Play time: 20 minutes of play, then a 20-minute break while Bip charges. Parents can change both.
