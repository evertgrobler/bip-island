Download for Mac

First install

  1. Double-click the downloaded zip. Drag Bip Island into your Applications folder.
  2. Right-click Bip Island and choose Open, then Open again.
    On macOS 15 or newer: if it says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. To leave the game: hold Esc for 3 seconds and answer the maths question.

After this, Bip Island updates itself.
