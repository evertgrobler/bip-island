Made for small hands

Nothing to read, nothing to rush

Every instruction is spoken

The narrator says what to do. Press any key to hear it again.

No timers, no lives

No countdowns and no "game over". A wrong answer gets a soft sound and another try.

A hint after two tries

Miss twice and Bip helps: counting along, or saying the sound again slowly.

Big buttons for a mouse

Every button is large enough for a four-year-old with a mouse. Stars earn stickers; nothing is ever bought.

Play-time breaks

After 20 minutes, Bip needs to charge

Bip's battery runs low, your child finishes the game they're in, and the islands rest for 20 minutes while Bip charges. Quitting and reopening the game doesn't skip the break.

  • Each child has their own clock. While one rests, a brother or sister can play.
  • You set the times: play length, break length and a daily maximum.
  • You can end a break early from behind the grown-up gate.

For grown-ups

The game stays a game

Bip Island opens full screen with the child lock on. To leave, or to open the settings, a grown-up holds Esc for 3 seconds, then answers a maths question or enters a passcode if you set one.

Child lock

Children can't switch away or quit by accident. On a Mac the Dock, menu bar, Cmd-Tab and Cmd-Q are blocked; on Windows the Windows key and Alt-Tab are. While the game is in front, the mouse stays on its screen, even with a second screen plugged in. Shutting down, and Ctrl-Alt-Del on Windows, always work.

Up to 4 children

Each child picks their animal picture on "Who's playing?" and has their own progress, stickers and play clock. Their age sets where they start.

Progress, sound by sound

Behind the gate you see each child's stars, minutes played today and this week, each skill and each phonics sound, and what needs more practice.

Nothing leaves the computer

No ads, in-app purchases, sign-up or tracking. Progress stays on your computer. Bip Island only goes online to check for updates, and a grown-up installs them.

Follows the Cambridge curriculum

Games line up with Cambridge Early Years and Primary Stages 1 to 3 for English, Mathematics and Computing.

Calm music, your choice

Gentle music plays on the map and the islands, never during a game. Set it to on, quiet or off.

Download for Mac

What you need

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
  • Any Mac with an Apple chip (M-series, or the A-series chip in a MacBook Neo) or an Intel processor
  • 4 GB of memory and about 500 MB of free space
  • Only a trackpad? Turn on Tap to click (System Settings → Trackpad) and tap-to-drag (System Settings → Accessibility → Pointer Control → Trackpad Options → Dragging style), because several games drag things around.

First install

  1. Open the downloaded BipIsland-mac.dmg and drag Bip Island into Applications. If an older Bip Island is already there, choose Replace.
  2. Open Bip Island. The Mac warns that it can't check the app: click Done (or OK).
  3. Within the hour, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Bip Island and enter your Mac's password. When the warning shows once more, click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 Sonoma you can instead right-click Bip Island, choose Open, then Open again.
Or install without the warning

Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), paste this line and press Return. It downloads the same game, puts it in Applications (replacing an older one) and opens it. A download made this way isn't marked as coming from the internet, so the Mac doesn't stop it.

curl -fL "{{MAC_URL}}" -o /tmp/BipIsland-mac.dmg && hdiutil attach -nobrowse -quiet -mountroot /tmp /tmp/BipIsland-mac.dmg && rm -rf "/Applications/Bip Island.app" && cp -R "/tmp/Bip Island/Bip Island.app" /Applications/ && hdiutil detach -quiet "/tmp/Bip Island" && open "/Applications/Bip Island.app"

Version {{VERSION}}, {{DATE}} · about 120 MB

Download for Windows

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Graphics that support OpenGL 3.3 (almost every PC from 2012 onwards)
  • 4 GB of memory and about 500 MB of free space

First install

  1. Open the downloaded BipIsland-win-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. You only see this once.
  3. Bip Island installs and opens by itself.
Or install without the warning

Right-click the Start button, choose Terminal (or Windows PowerShell), paste this line and press Enter. It downloads the same installer and runs it. A download made this way isn't marked as coming from the internet, so Windows doesn't stop it.

$ProgressPreference = "SilentlyContinue"; $f = "$env:TEMP\BipIsland-win-Setup.exe"; Invoke-WebRequest "{{WIN_URL}}" -OutFile $f -UseBasicParsing; Start-Process $f

Why the warning? Bip Island is made for our family and isn't sold, so it isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. Its updates are still checked before they install.

  • You'll need speakers or headphones (every instruction is spoken), a mouse (easier for small hands than a trackpad) and a keyboard for the grown-ups. The internet is only needed to download the game and its updates.
  • When an update is ready, an "Update ready" button appears for a grown-up to install.
  • The first time it opens, a short setup guide shows you how to reach the grown-up area and set up your children.
  • To leave the game: hold Esc for 3 seconds, answer the maths question (or enter your passcode), then choose Quit in Settings.
  • For a normal window without the lock, hold Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) while the game starts.
