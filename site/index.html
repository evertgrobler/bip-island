Bip Island

A learning game for children aged 4 to 8.

Children explore four islands with Bip, a small, friendly robot: Letters & Sounds, Numbers, Words & Spelling, and Coding.

A warm, friendly narrator speaks every instruction, so your child doesn't need to read to play. They pick what to play on the map, and Bip suggests what to try next.

Download the game

Four islands to explore

Each island has its own games. Rounds are made fresh every time, so the games don't repeat, and each game has levels that move up as your child gets things right. There are 11 games so far.

Letters & Sounds

Hear it, find it, write it

Children learn letter sounds first ("sss", not "ess"), in the order schools use: s a t p i n, then m d g o c k, and on through the digraphs. They meet each sound, hunt for pictures that start with it, pop the right letter and trace it with the mouse.

  • Meet the Sound
  • Sound Hunt
  • Bubble Pop
  • Letter Trace
  • Feed the Monster
Numbers

Counting real things

Maths starts with things you can see: tap each one as the narrator counts aloud, and learn to spot small amounts at a glance on dice and dots, before moving on to bigger numbers.

  • Count & Tap
  • Quick Look
Words & Spelling

From sounds to words

Children put the sounds they know together. They tap each sound in a word and hear it blend, then build words from letter tiles to match a picture. Only words made of sounds your child has already learnt are used.

  • Sound Buttons
  • Word Builder
Coding

First steps in programming, no reading needed

Coding starts with no code at all: putting picture cards in order, like the steps of getting ready in the morning. Then children snap arrow blocks together to walk Bip to his battery, and watch him follow their program step by step.

  • Morning Order
  • Bip's Path

For parents

Made for our own family first, with a few firm rules.

Kid lock

The game fills the screen and children can't switch away or quit by accident. On a Mac the Dock, menu bar, Cmd-Tab and Cmd-Q are blocked; on Windows the Windows key and Alt-Tab are. Shutting down, and Ctrl-Alt-Del on Windows, always work. To leave or open the settings, a grown-up holds Esc for 3 seconds and answers a maths question, or enters a passcode if you set one.

Play-time breaks

After 20 minutes, Bip's battery runs low. Your child finishes the game they're on, then Bip charges for 20 minutes and the games stay closed. Quitting and reopening doesn't skip it. You can change both times, set a daily limit, or end a break early.

Parent area and progress

Behind the parent gate you can see each child's stars and stickers, minutes played today and this week, how they're doing in each skill and each phonics sound, and what needs more practice.

Up to 4 children

Each child has their own progress and picks their animal picture on "Who's playing?". You add, rename or remove children in the parent area, and their age sets where they start.

No ads, no accounts, no data collection

No ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-up and no tracking. Progress stays on your computer. The only time Bip Island goes online is to check for updates, and a grown-up installs them behind the parent gate.

Follows the Cambridge curriculum

Games line up with Cambridge Early Years and Primary Stages 1 to 3 for English, Mathematics and Computing. There are no timers, lives or "game over": a wrong answer gets a gentle sound, another try, and a spoken hint after two misses.

First install

  1. Open the downloaded BipIsland-mac.dmg and drag Bip Island into Applications.
    If an older Bip Island is already there, choose Replace. Progress from the older version doesn't carry over.
  2. Right-click Bip Island and choose Open, then Open again.
    On macOS 15 or newer: if it says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Bip Island for Windows

Download for Windows

Version {{VERSION}}, {{DATE}}

First install

  1. Open the downloaded BipIsland-win-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This is needed once, because the game isn't signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Bip Island installs and opens by itself.

After that, on both

  • Bip Island finds its own updates. When one is ready, an "Update ready" button appears for a grown-up to install.
  • To leave the game: hold Esc for 3 seconds, answer the maths question (or enter your passcode), then choose Quit in Settings.
  • The game opens full screen with the kid lock on. For a normal window without the lock, hold Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) while it starts.
